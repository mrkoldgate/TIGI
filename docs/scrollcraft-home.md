# Homepage scroll score (scrollcraft)

Built with the scroll-craft skill (https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft) and its engine, vendored in `src/vendor/scrollcraft/`.
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("install this to improve the scroll animation on the website").

## Journey and feeling curve
| Act | Beat | Feeling, and what causes it | Device |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header film | Recognition | wonder: the land, the homes, the logo | film (autoplay, unchanged) |
| Intro | Promise | reassured: "own property in Africa, safely" | flow |
| Problem | Tension | unease: the question holds still while three sourced figures land one at a time | **pin** + count |
| Products | Turn | relief: three answers, the photos wipe into their frames in turn | **reveal** (wipe up) |
| Verify | Substance, **the peak** | confidence: the steps advance while the sample title report checks itself row by row and flips to Clear | **pin** + signature move, longest span (4.2 vh) |
| Markets | Range | breadth: the photo rail travels sideways under vertical scroll | **pan** |
| Shares | Substance | detail, at the reader's pace | flow |
| Legacy | Warmth | the family home opens out of a point | **reveal** (iris, once per page) |
| Intelligence → Close | Commitment | calm, readable, one action (Join the waitlist) | flow |

The peak, as a visitor would say it: "the title report checks itself while you scroll."

## Rules kept
- Only real numbers count up (the three sourced figures). No invented stats.
- No scroll cue in the hero (removed). One iris per page. Only the last act may hold a cue.
- Phones: the Verify act unpins and reads as a page (it does not fit one screen); the report still checks itself as it passes.
  Product wipes are desktop only. Reduced motion: the engine collapses movement, cues still fade, the rail becomes a native scroller.
- Brand rules win over the skill's refuse list where they conflict (TIGI's violet glow and glass are brand).
